### Exits Based on Momentum Exhaustion
* **The Concept:** Parabolic moves don't last forever. When a stock goes vertical, shooting up 40%, 50%, 60% in just a few days with massive volume, that's often the end, not the beginning.
* **Signs of Exhaustion:** You exit when you see signs of exhaustion, huge volume with little price progress, long upper wicks, back-to-back red days after a massive run. This is when the retail piles up the stock, and you should look to do the opposite. Qullamaggie even uses this setup to short stocks (we don’t have the liberty to do so in india, but we can use this as an exit technique).

#### Handling Ambiguity: The "Litmus Test"
Real-world charts rarely give the "textbook" volume climax shown in educational slides. When the volume signal is ambiguous—or when a stock simply drifts lower on low volume ("volume dry-up") instead of spiking—you cannot rely on volume alone to time the exit.

In scenarios where the trend is parabolic but volume is messy, the **Litmus Test** for momentum exhaustion is strictly **Price Action relative to the Moving Average (MA)**.

**The Litmus Test: The "10-Day Surrender"**
Since this strategy references **Qullamaggie**, the specific litmus test when volume is unclear is the **10-Day Moving Average Rule**.

* **The Rule:** If the stock is in a parabolic run (up 30-70%+ in a short time) but lacks a clear volume climax, you do **not** sell just because it "looks" high. You sell when the price **closes below the 10-Day Simple Moving Average (SMA)** (or 10/20 EMA depending on your aggression).
* **Why it works:** Parabolic moves respect the 10-Day MA like a law of physics. As long as the price stays above this line, the trend is technically intact, even if volume looks weak. A close below it confirms the momentum is definitively broken.

#### Analysis Checklist (Correction vs. Exhaustion)

| Feature | **Correction (Hold)** | **Exhaustion (Exit)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price vs. 10/20 MA** | Price touches or wicks below the MA but **closes above** it. | Price prints a full candle body **closed below** the MA. |
| **Candle Shape** | Small bodies, "resting" candles, inside bars. | **"Railroad Tracks"** (Long Green followed immediately by Long Red) or massive upper wicks (Shooting Stars). |
| **Extension** | Price is close to the MA lines (mean reversion happened). | Price is **extended** (20-30%+) far above the MA lines ("Air" between price and MA). |
| **RSI / Momentum** | RSI resets from 80+ down to 60-50 (cooling off). | **Bearish Divergence** (Price makes a higher high, but RSI makes a lower high). |

**Summary Checklist:**
1. **Check Extension:** Is there significant "white space" between the current price and the rising Moving Averages? (If yes -> High Risk).
2. **Check Divergence:** Look at the RSI (if added) or MACD. Is momentum dropping while price is rising?
3. **The Trigger (Litmus Test):** Wait for the **Close below the 10-Day MA**.

**Recommendation:** In the absence of a volume climax, strip away the noise and use the **10-Day MA Close** as your hard binary trigger. This prevents you from selling too early during a "quiet" grind up, but gets you out immediately when the trend structure breaks.
