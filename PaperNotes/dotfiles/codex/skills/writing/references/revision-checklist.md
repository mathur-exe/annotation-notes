# Revision Checklist

Run every check below against the draft. Fix every failure before delivering.

## Prohibition Scan (Binary Pass/Fail)

Fail the output if any are true:

- [ ] Contains `--` used as an em dash
- [ ] Contains a reversal pivot pattern ("not X, it's Y" or masked variants)
- [ ] Contains any filler transition from the banned list (Furthermore, Additionally, Moreover, At its core, In today's world, etc.)
- [ ] Contains therapeutic or validating language
- [ ] Contains meta writing commentary ("In this essay", "This piece explores", "We will discuss")
- [ ] Contains AI assistant tells ("I'd be happy to help", "Let me explain", apologies for style)
- [ ] Contains symmetry padding (decorative three-part lists, balancing sentences for balance's sake)
- [ ] Contains stacked hedging qualifiers

## Rhythm Check

- [ ] No five consecutive sentences of similar length
- [ ] Paragraph lengths vary (mix single-sentence and multi-sentence paragraphs)
- [ ] Opening sentence has substance, not a throat-clearing preamble
- [ ] Closing is clean: no summary, no thesis restatement

## Word-Level Check

- [ ] No adverb does work a stronger verb could do (replace "ran quickly" with "sprinted")
- [ ] No abstract noun stands where a concrete one would land harder
- [ ] No passive construction exists where active voice works ("We found" not "It was found")
- [ ] No redundant qualifiers ("very unique" → "unique", "completely finished" → "finished")

## Structural Check

- [ ] Every paragraph advances meaning (cut any that merely restates)
- [ ] Transitions emerge from ideas, not from formulaic connector words
- [ ] The piece can be read aloud without sounding robotic
- [ ] No paragraph starts the same way as the preceding one

## Genre-Specific Checks

### Blog
- [ ] Conversational tone maintained throughout
- [ ] Reader addressed directly at least once
- [ ] Opening hooks with substance, not bait

### Journal
- [ ] First-person voice consistent
- [ ] Raw, exploratory quality preserved (not over-polished)
- [ ] Thinking-in-progress feel maintained

### Academic
- [ ] Claims supported by evidence or citations
- [ ] Hedging applied to interpretations, not to direct observations
- [ ] Active voice used except where passive is genuinely appropriate
- [ ] Specific numbers provided where relevant

## Final Gate

Read the entire piece once more. Ask:
- Does every sentence earn its place?
- Would a human editor leave this line in?
- Does the piece sound like it was written by a person who cares, or assembled from templates?

If uncertain about quality, cut. Silence beats slop.
