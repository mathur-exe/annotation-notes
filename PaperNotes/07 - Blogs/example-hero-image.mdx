- **!(2025-12-25) Self-Worth Theory**

  - In continuation to [[!(2025-12-24) The Collector's Fallacy]] this is a classical psychological phenomenon often plagues "high-potential" individuals particularly those who come through high-pressure systems like JEE.
    <Sidenote>From a psychological lens, it is a self-defence mechanism.</Sidenote>

  - 1. Equation of Performance-Ability-Worth
    - $Performance = Ability = Self-worth$

  - 2. Cognitive Friction of active recall
    - The primary reason for my procrastination is a combination of friction and fear of active recall.
      <Sidenote>From a psychological POV, this pattern emerged during the JEE days when each active recall / test were about ranking, consequence, identity, future anxiety.</Sidenote>
      
    <Figure 
      src="https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation/images/chess.png" 
      caption="Figure 2: Wide image flowing into margins." 
      wide 
    />

    - There are useful frameworks which can redefine friction on active recall and tests:
      - **Method 1: Redefine success:** instead of binary scoring recall attempts, monolog must focus on *"I attempted recall despite discomfort"*
      - **Method 2: Debrief line:** after recall brain must learn:
        - (1) Gaps in knowledge,
        - (2) Forgetting is expected,
        - (3) This is how memory is built

- **!(2025-12-24) The Collector's Fallacy**

  - ==Getting Your Dopamine From Information Gathering==: **dopamine from information is dangerous drug. Your entire life will change the moment you start acting on the information you already have. Get your dopamine into action.**

  - IMO: **Aimless reading isn't bad, curiosity without an agenda is not a bug; it’s how polymaths are made.** Many of the most interesting intellectual leaps come from exactly this kind of wandering. So the flaw is not "reading without a goal."

    - Kinds of reading:
      - (1) Exploratory (healthy)
      - (2) Dopaminergic (the trap)

    - Other thoughts:
      - The missing piece: assimilation pressure
        - This means non-action forms can help like:
          - (1) rewrite idea in own primitive language
          - (2) arguing against it without external help
          - (3) linking the idea to something already known
      - Information may not have action item but ==Epistemic action== is must like (1) updating belief system
      - The danger of endless reading isn't wasted time, rather the feeling of continuity of thought without actual accumulation of insight
